Privacy Policy

App: Himwellness TikTok Creative Tool (internal utility by Himwellness Sdn Bhd, Malaysia).

Data we collect

  • Via TikTok Login/Display API after you authorize: basic profile (open ID, display name) and your public video list (video ID, title, create_time/post time, cover image, share URL).
  • We do not collect passwords, private messages, or other accounts' data.

How we use it

  • To display a bulk list of videos with post times for internal creative performance analysis.
  • Data stays local to our staff browser/session unless you export a CSV for internal reporting.

Sharing

No sale, no sharing with third parties. TikTok API data is used only as described here.

Storage & retention

Access tokens are kept in-memory/session only and refreshed via TikTok OAuth. You can revoke access anytime in TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

Contact / deletion

To request deletion of data, contact Himwellness Sdn Bhd via your account manager.
